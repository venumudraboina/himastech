# HIMASTECH — Digital Realty Marketing Website

An ultra-premium, high-converting creative website built for **HIMASTECH — Digital Realty Marketing** led by **Kishore Patel**, specializing in Real Estate, Digital Marketing, AI Marketing, Sales Training, and Business Growth solutions.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Features

1. **Luxury Visual Identity**:
   - Obsidian dark mode (`#07090e`) with metallic 24K Gold gradients and subtle Cyber-Cyan neon accents.
   - Frosted glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), smooth scroll, and glowing HUD interfaces.
   - Typography: Google Fonts **Outfit** (headings), **Plus Jakarta Sans** (body), and **JetBrains Mono** (dashboard metrics).

2. **17 Core Sections (from Concept Brief)**:
   - **Hero Section**: High-impact headline, live lead tickers, and floating HUD analytics cards.
   - **Trust Strip**: Live counters for 12+ Years Experience, ₹500Cr+ Real Estate Marketed, 150,000+ Leads, and 12x ROAS.
   - **The Himastech Difference**: Interactive 5-stage funnel (`ATTRACT ➔ ENGAGE ➔ LEADS ➔ SALES ➔ GROWTH`).
   - **Real Estate Marketing Powerhouse**: 12 core realty services, hyper-local Hyderabad targeting, and marketing plan configurator.
   - **AI Marketing Suite**: 5 AI pillars + **Interactive Real Estate AI Ad & Strategy Simulator** (generates real-time ad hooks & audience vectors).
   - **Digital Business Solutions**: Digital Visiting Card & WhatsApp Mini-Website with an **Interactive Smartphone Simulator**.
   - **Complete Digital Marketing Matrix**: Filterable grid across Paid Ads, Creative & Media, and Web & AI Technology.
   - **Your Growth Journey**: 6-step roadmap from Discovery to Scaling.
   - **Interactive ROI & Lead Calculator**: Live estimate of reach, qualified inquiries, CPL, site visits, and inventory revenue velocity.
   - **Creative Portfolio**: Filterable project case studies + **Interactive Draggable Before & After Split Comparison Slider**.
   - **About Kishore Patel**: Profile spotlight, 12+ years experience highlights, keynote training credentials, and 1-on-1 consult booking.
   - **Corporate & Team Training**: Real Estate sales mastery, AI automation for teams, and builder sales enablement.
   - **Who We Help**: Tailored matrices for Builders, Developers, Channel Partners, Local Businesses, Founders, and Startups.
   - **Live Marketing Dashboard Preview**: Real-time HUD showing active campaigns, qualified leads, and micro-market lead velocity.
   - **Client Testimonials**: Verified reviews from leading Hyderabad developers.
   - **Powerful CTA & Contact Section**: Multi-step booking form, direct WhatsApp integration, phone lines, and Hyderabad HQ address.
   - **Mobile Conversion Experience**: Fixed bottom conversion bar (`CALL` | `WHATSAPP` | `GET STARTED`) & floating WhatsApp widget.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can open `index.html` directly in any web browser, or run a local web server:

### Option 1: Python
```bash
python -m http.server 8080
```
Then open: [http://localhost:8080/](http://localhost:8080/)

### Option 2: Node.js / npx
```bash
npx serve .
```
